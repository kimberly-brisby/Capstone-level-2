Insights into Homeschooling from a Former Educator's Viewpoint

An aid to parents rather seasoned or not!

What comes to your mind when thinking of Homeschooling?

  • Pajamas?
  • Sleeping in late?
  • Learnig with no struture?
  • Religous-based?

Homeschool in simple terms is school in a non-tradational brick and morter building, in other words school at home. However it is much more to this very simple yet complex system.

There are many reasons that parent/caregiver(s) decide to homeschool there child(ren) and as long as the child(ren) are safe there is no right or wrong way of doing so. This page is for the parent/caregiver(s) that are considerig going down this road. Journey with me as a former educator on the information highway of some benefits and obstacles to and for homeschooling children in today's era.

It can be a thrill of a challenge to homeschool. There are dfferent methods and each state has there own rules and regulations to be followed. The most imporant thing is to find the version tat works best for you; rather that is based off your religion, following the districts model, unschooling, or even a mix of them all.
